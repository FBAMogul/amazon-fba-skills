# Amazon Ads reports (`ads_reports`)

The advertising reports a seller gets from the Amazon Ads console or the Amazon Ads API: what Sponsored Products ads cost, the clicks they got and the sales Amazon credits to them, by campaign, placement, target, search term and product. Sponsored Brands and Sponsored Display are covered briefly. Ads data doesn't come through the Selling Partner API (SP-API); the Amazon Ads API is a separate API with its own sign-up ([Ads API onboarding](https://d3a0d0y2hgofx6.cloudfront.net/en-us/guides/onboarding/overview.md)).

**Written from Amazon's documentation (28 Oct 2026).** Unlike most pages in this pack, the columns here were not checked against a real download or API file, so treat every column name as Amazon's description, find columns by name, and say so when a file differs. The `d3a0d0y2hgofx6.cloudfront.net` links are the raw-markdown copies of the Ads API docs that Amazon's [llms.txt](https://advertising.amazon.com/API/docs/en-us/llms.txt) points to.

## Where to find it in Seller Central

Checked in Seller Central UK on 28 Oct 2026: ads reports live in the Ads console, which Seller Central links to. New Seller Central: **Marketing > Ads console** (`/cm`). Classic: **Advertising > Campaign Manager**, and **Reports > Advertising Reports**, which opens the Ads console ([seller-central-map](seller-central-map.md)). The Ads console's own menus weren't checked; the paths below are from Amazon's Ads help pages. In the Ads console:

- **Unified reporting** (the new reports): **Reporting** in the left-hand menu ([Create a report in unified reporting](https://advertising.amazon.com/help/GK3FG568T93VE2VL)); Amazon's announcement calls it **Measurement and Reporting > Reporting**. Generally available since 8 Jun 2026, the UK included ([announcement](https://advertising.amazon.com/resources/whats-new/streamline-campaign-analysis-with-unified-reporting)). Build a report or start from a template (Advertised product, Campaign, Converted product, Placement, Search term, Search term impression share, Targeting, Top-of-search impression share and more). A report needs at least one time dimension, one level of detail (account, campaign, ad group or ad) and one metric. It runs once, or daily, weekly or monthly ([Understand unified reporting](https://advertising.amazon.com/help/GMH8A8AJSH4ATV6T); [Create a report](https://advertising.amazon.com/help/GK3FG568T93VE2VL)).
- **Sponsored ads reports** (the older reports): **Measurement and reporting > Sponsored Ads reports** ([announcement](https://advertising.amazon.com/resources/whats-new/streamline-campaign-analysis-with-unified-reporting)). **From 17 Dec 2026 new reports can't be created and existing ones can't be edited** (they stay readable), **and they shut down on 31 Dec 2026**; Amazon says these dates may change. After 31 Dec, "all saved reports, scheduled reports, and historical data still on these pages will be permanently deleted". A migration tool moves scheduled reports to unified reporting; after migrating, earlier runs can be downloaded from History in the report's Actions menu ([Migrate your scheduled reports](https://advertising.amazon.com/help/GK3JZZE5QLTF8U8A)). Amazon recommends downloading and saving reports monthly ([Downloadable reports](https://advertising.amazon.com/help/GBYSPTSLR337JMLH)).
- **Invoices and payments:** **Administration > Billing and Payment > Billing**. Invoices sit under Documents, each with a PDF and **Download CSV**; **Download summary** gives a CSV of invoices from the last 90 days. **Payment settings** shows the payment method ([View and understand your invoice](https://advertising.amazon.com/help/GDWV9R4RA7FCHSTE)).

Amazon's Sponsored Products methodology page says the interface and bulk reports export as .csv ([methodology](https://advertising.amazon.com/help/G8KL93RTVG44RZAQ)). File names and exact headers weren't checked.

**Sponsored Brands and Sponsored Display** (Amazon's help now calls the second one Display) need the seller to be enrolled in Brand Registry ([profiles](https://d3a0d0y2hgofx6.cloudfront.net/en-us/guides/account-management/authorization/profiles.md)). Their console reports include Campaign, Keyword, Search term and Attributed purchases (Sponsored Brands) and Campaign, Targeting, Advertised product, Purchased product and Matched target (Display) ([Downloadable reports](https://advertising.amazon.com/help/GBYSPTSLR337JMLH)). Through Ads API v3 they keep 60 days (Sponsored Brands; 731 for its purchased product report) and 65 days (Sponsored Display) ([v3 campaign](https://d3a0d0y2hgofx6.cloudfront.net/en-us/guides/reporting/v3/report-types/campaign.md); [v3 purchased product](https://d3a0d0y2hgofx6.cloudfront.net/en-us/guides/reporting/v3/report-types/purchased-product.md)). Their click-attributed sales use a 14-day window, views on vCPM campaigns follow Amazon's 2026 view attribution rules, and their sales can include sales by Amazon or other sellers (see How the figures are counted).

## Getting it through the API

- **A separate API with its own sign-up.** Create a Login with Amazon (LwA) security profile on developer.amazon.com, a new one even if you already use the SP-API. Apply for Ads API access as a **Direct Advertiser** (review "may take up to 1 business day"), then assign the access to the LwA profile from Amazon's approval email. That link "cannot be changed once it is set", and Amazon allows one client ID per company ([create LwA](https://d3a0d0y2hgofx6.cloudfront.net/en-us/guides/onboarding/create-lwa-app.md); [apply](https://d3a0d0y2hgofx6.cloudfront.net/en-us/guides/onboarding/apply-for-access.md); [assign](https://d3a0d0y2hgofx6.cloudfront.net/en-us/guides/onboarding/assign-api-access.md)).
- **Credentials:** the client ID, an access token made from a refresh token, and a profile ID, for v3 reports and invoices ([getting started](https://d3a0d0y2hgofx6.cloudfront.net/en-us/guides/get-started/overview.md)). Reporting API v1 takes the advertiser account ID in the request body instead (`accessRequestedAccounts` > `advertiserAccountId`); `POST /adsApi/v1/query/advertiserAccounts` lists accounts with their profile IDs ([v1 quickstart](https://d3a0d0y2hgofx6.cloudfront.net/en-us/guides/reporting/ads-v1/quickstart.md); [advertiser accounts](https://d3a0d0y2hgofx6.cloudfront.net/en-us/guides/account-management/query-advertiser-accounts.md)). Amazon's v3 guide also lists an `Amazon-Ads-AccountId` header as required for report requests, though its own sample calls leave it out ([v3 get started](https://d3a0d0y2hgofx6.cloudfront.net/en-us/guides/reporting/v3/get-started.md)). For the UK and EU stores: authorisation at `https://eu.account.amazon.com/ap/oa`, tokens from `https://api.amazon.co.uk/auth/o2/token`, and API calls to `https://advertising-api-eu.amazon.com` ([auth grant](https://d3a0d0y2hgofx6.cloudfront.net/en-us/guides/get-started/create-authorization-grant.md); [tokens](https://d3a0d0y2hgofx6.cloudfront.net/en-us/guides/get-started/retrieve-access-token.md); [endpoints](https://d3a0d0y2hgofx6.cloudfront.net/en-us/reference/api-overview.md)). Keep them out of the chat as for the SP-API keys ([getting-access](getting-access.md)).
- **One profile per store.** `GET /v2/profiles` lists them. With a View-only user (see Read-only use below), add `?accessLevel=view&apiProgram=report`, because by default it lists only profiles the user can view and edit ([permissions](https://d3a0d0y2hgofx6.cloudfront.net/en-us/guides/account-management/permissions.md)). Each has its own currency and time zone: UK `GBP` and `Europe/London`; DE, FR, IT and ES `EUR` and `Europe/Paris` ([profiles API](https://d3a0d0y2hgofx6.cloudfront.net/en-us/profiles/3-0/openapi.yaml)).
- **v3 reports** (shut off after 30 Jun 2027): `POST /reporting/reports` with `adProduct` `SPONSORED_PRODUCTS`, a `reportTypeId`, `groupBy`, the `columns` wanted, `timeUnit` `DAILY` or `SUMMARY`, and format `GZIP_JSON`; then `GET /reporting/reports/{reportId}` until the status is `COMPLETED` and download the file at `url`, a gzip of JSON. At most 31 days per request. A report can take up to three hours; an identical request while one runs gets `425`; Amazon suggests 1 or 2 requests per report type per day ([v3 get started](https://d3a0d0y2hgofx6.cloudfront.net/en-us/guides/reporting/v3/get-started.md); [v3 FAQ](https://d3a0d0y2hgofx6.cloudfront.net/en-us/guides/reporting/v3/faq.md); [reporting overview](https://d3a0d0y2hgofx6.cloudfront.net/en-us/guides/reporting/overview.md)).
- **Reporting API v1** (the newer one): `POST /adsApi/v1/create/reports` with the advertiser account ID, a date period and a list of `fields` (dimensions such as `campaign.id` and metrics such as `metric.clicks`; there are no report types), then `POST /adsApi/v1/retrieve/reports` about once a minute until `COMPLETED` ([v1 quickstart](https://d3a0d0y2hgofx6.cloudfront.net/en-us/guides/reporting/ads-v1/quickstart.md)). Format `CSV` or `GZIP_JSON`, or `PARTITIONED_CSV` or `PARTITIONED_GZIP_JSON` for large reports, which come as several files of up to 1 GB each ([v1 quickstart](https://d3a0d0y2hgofx6.cloudfront.net/en-us/guides/reporting/ads-v1/quickstart.md); [v1 new features](https://d3a0d0y2hgofx6.cloudfront.net/en-us/guides/reporting/ads-v1/new-features.md)). One report runs at a time ([v1 overview](https://d3a0d0y2hgofx6.cloudfront.net/en-us/guides/reporting/ads-v1/overview.md)).
- **Invoices:** `GET /invoices` and `GET /invoices/{invoiceId}` give each invoice's amount due and tax ([invoices](https://d3a0d0y2hgofx6.cloudfront.net/en-us/guides/account-management/billing/invoices.md)). Campaign names and budgets as they stand now come from exports, which Amazon recommends over putting names in reports ([v3 FAQ](https://d3a0d0y2hgofx6.cloudfront.net/en-us/guides/reporting/v3/faq.md)).
- **Read-only use.** The scope Amazon grants, `advertising::campaign_management`, covers changing campaigns too ([assign](https://d3a0d0y2hgofx6.cloudfront.net/en-us/guides/onboarding/assign-api-access.md)). So call only profiles, reports, exports and invoices, never the campaign, ad group, keyword, target, bid or budget endpoints. For a safety net, authorise with a Seller Central user who has only View access to Campaign manager, Product Ads Performance Reports and Product Ads Invoice History; Amazon says those seller permissions decide what an API request may do ([permissions](https://d3a0d0y2hgofx6.cloudfront.net/en-us/guides/account-management/permissions.md)). Not confirmed: Amazon doesn't say whether View alone lets an app create reports, so try one report first.
- **How far back Amazon goes:**
  - Unified reporting and Reporting API v1: 24 months by day or week, 72 months by month or year, 14 days by hour. One report can cover at most 120 days by day, 15 months by week, or 25 months by month ([Data availability](https://advertising.amazon.com/help/G8A5Z6UD9ME5W3GZ); [v1 dates](https://d3a0d0y2hgofx6.cloudfront.net/en-us/guides/reporting/ads-v1/dates.md)). Amazon raised daily and weekly history from 15 to 24 months on 26 Aug 2026; the 8 Jun 2026 announcement still says 15. The longest range one report can cover didn't change ([release notes](https://d3a0d0y2hgofx6.cloudfront.net/en-us/release-notes/index.md)).
  - The advertised product and converted product dimensions only start on 13 Feb 2025; earlier dates show them empty ([Data availability](https://advertising.amazon.com/help/G8A5Z6UD9ME5W3GZ), which covers the Reporting API too; the v1 dates page lists only the benchmark dimensions).
  - Ads API v3 and the older console reports: per report, in the table above.

## Columns

Names differ by surface. The console's columns have been renamed, and Amazon's help shows the old names in brackets ("formerly known as"); v3 uses camelCase names; v1 uses `metric.` and dimension fields. Not checked against a real file: find each column by name and accept either the old or the new console name. Sources: the console help pages for each report (linked above), [v3 columns](https://d3a0d0y2hgofx6.cloudfront.net/en-us/guides/reporting/v3/columns.md), the [v3 to v1 field map](https://d3a0d0y2hgofx6.cloudfront.net/en-us/guides/amazon-marketing-stream/v2/report-type-migration-guide.md) and the v1 metric pages ([purchases and sales](https://d3a0d0y2hgofx6.cloudfront.net/en-us/guides/reporting/ads-v1/metrics/amazon-retail-conversions/purchases.md), [costs](https://d3a0d0y2hgofx6.cloudfront.net/en-us/guides/reporting/ads-v1/metrics/costs-and-fees/costs.md), [clicks](https://d3a0d0y2hgofx6.cloudfront.net/en-us/guides/reporting/ads-v1/metrics/delivery/clicks.md)).

## How the figures are counted

- **Attribution for a seller's Sponsored Products:** 7 days after a click, clicks only, last click. It counts the advertised products and "any other products sold by the Seller Central merchant". Sponsored Brands and Display: 14 days for clicks, and the brand's other products, which "may include sales by Amazon or third-party sellers, in New or Used condition". Views on vCPM-billed Sponsored Brands and Display campaigns are different: since 1 Jan 2026 (the UK included) their standard Purchases, Sales and ROAS use Amazon's "shopping-signal enhanced last-touch" model with a shorter view window, while unified reporting's separate "all views" metrics, such as `Purchases (all views)`, keep 14 days; don't compare or add the two as if they matched, or compare across that date without saying so ([View attribution updates](https://advertising.amazon.com/resources/whats-new/view-attribution-updates-for-amazon-store-ads/)). A sale is credited to one ad interaction at most, across all campaigns and advertisers, with one exception: sellers' Sponsored Products "doesn't normally compete for attribution with brands", so the same sale can also be credited to a brand's ad bought by a different advertiser (for one advertiser it's credited once). A seller who also runs Display, Video or Audio campaigns should enter their merchant token in those campaigns' account settings ([Ad campaign attribution](https://advertising.amazon.com/help/GX7KDKHMWQYMJ385); [v1 overview](https://d3a0d0y2hgofx6.cloudfront.net/en-us/guides/reporting/ads-v1/overview.md)).
- **Sales sit on the click date, not the order date.** A sale shown on 1 October came from a click on 1 October but may have happened up to the end of the window ([Ad campaign attribution](https://advertising.amazon.com/help/GX7KDKHMWQYMJ385)). A date's figures stay incomplete until its window has passed.
- **Not VAT, not discounts.** "Attributed sales don't include tax. Inclusive tax such as VAT is subtracted from sales totals", and they "don't reflect any discounts applied in the cart" ([Ad campaign attribution](https://advertising.amazon.com/help/GX7KDKHMWQYMJ385)). The report's `Cost` is before tax: the cost column "only shows the total click charges", and the invoice adds the taxes ([Understand campaign spend](https://advertising.amazon.com/help/GUJY2CG5DTCPDCVQ)). VAT on ad charges depends on the account's address and VAT registration when the invoice is made, and the invoice shows it ([Update billing details](https://advertising.amazon.com/help/GH9WLA7VXX3UR92L); [Tax refunds](https://advertising.amazon.com/help/GBM34HJ5MUHACVJM)).
- **Recent days change.** Clicks and impressions reach the API within 12 hours; invalid traffic is watched for up to 30 days, and Amazon recommends waiting at least 5 days before judging clicks. Conversion data can change for up to 60 days, so pull reports again monthly ([reporting overview](https://d3a0d0y2hgofx6.cloudfront.net/en-us/guides/reporting/overview.md)). Invalid traffic is taken out of the reports' clicks ([Downloadable reports](https://advertising.amazon.com/help/GBYSPTSLR337JMLH)) and isn't billed ([Gross and invalid traffic](https://advertising.amazon.com/help/GSH9JG8Q38TMWGL5)). The v3 FAQ adds that conversions are restated 1, 7 and 28 days after the sale ([v3 FAQ](https://d3a0d0y2hgofx6.cloudfront.net/en-us/guides/reporting/v3/faq.md)). Orders cancelled, or whose payment failed, within 72 hours are taken off, which can take up to 7 days ([Ad campaign attribution](https://advertising.amazon.com/help/GX7KDKHMWQYMJ385)). Amazon calls Sponsored Products click data "official and final" after 30 days, with rare revisions up to 90 days ([methodology](https://advertising.amazon.com/help/G8KL93RTVG44RZAQ)).
- **Time zone:** "Reporting is provided in the time zone of the marketplace in which the campaign is set up" ([methodology](https://advertising.amazon.com/help/G8KL93RTVG44RZAQ)): `Europe/London` for UK campaigns ([profiles API](https://d3a0d0y2hgofx6.cloudfront.net/en-us/profiles/3-0/openapi.yaml)). A downloaded report shows the time zone the account is registered under ([Downloadable reports](https://advertising.amazon.com/help/GBYSPTSLR337JMLH)). In v1, each row's day is the campaign's own time zone, so a report across stores mixes zones ([v1 dates](https://d3a0d0y2hgofx6.cloudfront.net/en-us/guides/reporting/ads-v1/dates.md)).
- **Currency:** each store's campaigns in its own currency, and ad charges are made "in the currency of the marketplace where the ads were created" ([Payment methods](https://advertising.amazon.com/help/GMELNQQNEEVLX456)).

## How ads data meets the other reports

- **How a seller pays:** by card, from the seller account, or by monthly invoice. From the seller account, charges come out of Funds Available, "not your Total Balance" ([Payment methods](https://advertising.amazon.com/help/GMELNQQNEEVLX456)). An account upgraded to an Amazon Ads advertiser account must pay by invoice for all campaigns, sponsored ads included; Amazon announced on 30 Jul 2026 that it is upgrading existing Amazon DSP accounts ([Payment methods](https://advertising.amazon.com/help/GMELNQQNEEVLX456); [advertiser account announcement](https://advertising.amazon.com/en-gb/resources/whats-new/manage-ad-products-globally-with-advertiser-account)). Amazon told a small number of advertisers it would move them to the seller balance or Pay by Invoice by default, keeping the card as a backup ([payment method update](https://advertising.amazon.com/help/GF2FM96W5SE8HYK6)); on 14 Apr 2026 it put that back to 1 Aug 2026 ([Update on advertiser payments](https://advertising.amazon.com/library/news/update-on-advertiser-payments)), so check the seller's own billing settings and any notice from Amazon rather than assuming which method a month used. When and how often invoices come depends on the method. Paid by card or from the seller account, an invoice is raised each time charges reach the credit limit, and otherwise on the second day of the next month ([billing cycles](https://advertising.amazon.com/help/GY3YNTWRZYVPLADL)); Amazon's FAQ says the third ([Ads FAQ](https://advertising.amazon.com/en-gb/resources/faq)). With Pay by Invoice, there's one invoice at the end of each month, due 30 days later ([Update on advertiser payments](https://advertising.amazon.com/library/news/update-on-advertiser-payments)).
- **In the payment reports:** paid from the seller account, the deductions show in Transaction View "with 'Cost of Advertising' description" ([payment method update](https://advertising.amazon.com/help/GF2FM96W5SE8HYK6)); in new Seller Central Transaction View is under Finance > Finance reports ([transactions](transactions.md)). The Finances API has a "Sponsored Products payment event" with the invoice id, `baseValue` (before tax), `taxValue` and `transactionValue` ([Finances v0 model](https://github.com/amzn/selling-partner-api-models/blob/main/models/finances-api-model/financesV0.json)); [transactions](transactions.md) lists a `ProductAdsPayment` transaction type. Amazon's settlement report docs don't say how ad charges appear in [settlements](settlements.md). "1 invoice may cover multiple settlement periods" ([Understand campaign spend](https://advertising.amazon.com/help/GUJY2CG5DTCPDCVQ)).
- **Per SKU:** Seller Economics carries ad charges (`ads[]` in [seller_economics](seller_economics.md)), and its net proceeds take them off ([schema](https://github.com/amzn/selling-partner-api-models/blob/main/schemas/data-kiosk/analytics_economics_2024_03_15.graphql)). Only Sponsored Products charges are supported there; each charge's total is amount minus promotion plus tax ([SKU Economics help](https://sellercentral.amazon.co.uk/help/hub/reference/external/GZ8Y22NL2FSRY8M5)).
- **Don't take ad spend off twice.** Ads paid from the seller account are already deducted in the Transactions totals and the settlements, so don't take the ads report's `Cost` off those again. Take off only the ads paid by card or invoice outside the account, at their invoice amounts, which already include the Regulatory Advertising Fee and the credits (check each invoice's status, as "Report cost and the invoice differ" says; with their VAT for a seller who can't reclaim it), not at `Cost`, as [seller_economics](seller_economics.md) says. Seller Economics' net proceeds already take off its `ads[]` charges too. The SKU Economics download can have no advertising column even with it ticked (see [seller_economics](seller_economics.md)): then, on SKUs the ads report shows spend for (on the rest the check proves nothing), check whether `Net proceeds total` equals `Net sales` minus the fee totals and any costs the seller entered (per-unit costs times net units). Ads are in only if the gap is about that SKU's ad spend; any other gap means a step is off, so find it before deciding. Say which you found. Count each fee once: use the fee totals, not their per-unit and component columns as well (see [seller_economics](seller_economics.md)). Those charges are before the VAT the invoice adds, and Amazon doesn't say whether they include a Regulatory Advertising Fee, so check the invoice before adding that fee as a cost, and for credits (invalid clicks, over-delivery, promotions) these charges may not reflect (see `ads[].charge` in [seller_economics](seller_economics.md)).
- **Joining ads to sales:** by advertised SKU to [orders](orders.md), or by ASIN to [sales_and_traffic](sales_and_traffic.md)'s `childAsin`. Halo sales in the purchased product report have an ASIN only.
- **Attributed sales are not total sales.** They leave out VAT, cart discounts and orders cancelled within 72 hours; they sit on the click date; they include halo products; and Amazon says they "may not always align with retail reports" ([Ad campaign attribution](https://advertising.amazon.com/help/GX7KDKHMWQYMJ385)). [sales_and_traffic](sales_and_traffic.md) and [orders](orders.md) include VAT and count by order date. So don't subtract ad sales from total sales and call the rest organic, as the two aren't on the same basis. Before dividing ad spend by total sales, put both on the same VAT basis (see the VAT section of [SKILL.md](../SKILL.md)) and say which dates each side uses.

## Personal data

None about buyers in the documented columns: every row is a total per campaign, target, search term or product, with no order ids, names or addresses. Customer search terms are shoppers' own words; Amazon doesn't say they are screened, so don't publish raw lists. The seller's campaign names, SKUs, bids and figures are their business. Invoices and the billing profile hold the seller's business name, address and VAT number ([billing and payment profiles](https://advertising.amazon.com/help/GUSV4U4L49UGCLNX)): keep them private. Unified reporting can email a report link, and "anyone is able to view the link" ([Create a report](https://advertising.amazon.com/help/GK3FG568T93VE2VL)), so only send it to the seller. Rule: strip personal data before analysis and never copy it into anything shared.

## Questions it answers

- What did ads cost per campaign and per month, and what sales did Amazon credit to them (ACOS, ROAS)?
- Which search terms took clicks but no orders (candidates for negative keywords), and which sold?
- Which advertised SKUs cost more in ads than their margin covers? (Profit before ads: work it out per SKU the way [seller_economics](seller_economics.md) says for the seller's VAT position, from net sales after refunds (`netProductSales`; `Net sales` in the download), less fees, the cost of goods and any other costs entered in SKU Central, such as shipping to Amazon; for a Flat Rate seller that page says this dataset can't give their profit, so work from [transactions](transactions.md) as it says, but leave out the `ProductAdsPayment` transactions and don't take off the ads invoices, as those are the ad cost compared below; the VAT they owe comes as one total for the period, so share it by each SKU's sales including VAT, or use their flat rate times those sales, and label it an estimate. The SKU Economics download has no fee VAT columns, so for a seller on standard VAT accounting take the fee VAT from `transactions`, or label the margin as after fees including VAT. Not net proceeds, which already take ads off. Real ad cost: each SKU's `Cost`, plus every invoice adjustment for the period that `Cost` doesn't already reflect, applied to the campaigns and SKUs it covers where the invoice says which (some credits are for one campaign or portfolio); where it doesn't, sharing it in proportion to `Cost` is only an estimate, so do it only if the seller asks and label it (see "Report cost and the invoice differ": any Regulatory Advertising Fee added, a cost for every seller; invalid clicks, over-delivery and promotional credits taken off), Then, only for a seller who can't reclaim VAT, add VAT at the invoice's rate on each SKU's ad cost (that part isn't an estimate). A period can have several invoices. Where any part isn't known, say the comparison is before it rather than calling the ads covered.)
- How much of the ad sales were other products (halo), and which ASINs?
- Do ads do better at top of search or on product pages (placement)?
- Which campaigns ran out of budget, and what did that cost in estimated missed sales (budget report: create it before 17 Dec 2026)?
- What did the ads invoices total in a month, and which seller-account deductions paid them?

## Traps from Amazon's documentation

- **The old console reports can't be created or edited from 17 Dec 2026, and they and their history are deleted after 31 Dec 2026** (Amazon says the dates may change; [Migrate your scheduled reports](https://advertising.amazon.com/help/GK3JZZE5QLTF8U8A)). Create and download the long-history ones the seller needs before 17 Dec, such as the 5-year Sponsored Products campaign report and the 2-year budget report: unified reporting keeps only 24 months by day. The Sponsored Products budget report, the Amazon Business placement report and the Sponsored Products prompt and video reports aren't in unified reporting yet, and migrating them fails. Amazon says prompts and video are due later in 2026, but gives no date for the other two ([Migrate your scheduled reports](https://advertising.amazon.com/help/GK3JZZE5QLTF8U8A); [Understand unified reporting](https://advertising.amazon.com/help/GMH8A8AJSH4ATV6T)).
- **Column names have changed,** from day-count names (`7 Day Total Sales`) to `Sales`, `Sales (promoted)` and `Sales (halo)`. A file from before the change uses the old names.
- **In Sponsored Products reports, the console's `14 Day …` columns aren't a seller's numbers** (Sponsored Brands and Display do use 14 days). In the Ads console, sellers' Sponsored Products sales use 7 days and the 14-day columns are vendors' (see the columns table). An Ads API v3 report can use any of the 1, 7, 14 or 30-day windows: keep the window it used and label it, and use 7 days to match the seller's console. Amazon's own console vs API table works out Sponsored Products ACOS and ROAS from 14-day sales ([console vs API](https://d3a0d0y2hgofx6.cloudfront.net/en-us/guides/reporting/v2/advertising-console.md)), so to match the console, recompute them from `Cost` and the 7-day sales in the same file.
- **Search term reports hold only terms with at least 1 click,** so their impressions don't match the campaign totals ([Search term help](https://advertising.amazon.com/help/G3HEFZYWZF84NPS9)). Amazon's v3 FAQ still says the console report also has terms with no clicks ([v3 FAQ](https://d3a0d0y2hgofx6.cloudfront.net/en-us/guides/reporting/v3/faq.md)); the current help page says it doesn't.
- **The purchased product report won't add up to the campaign totals:** it holds only the halo sales ([Purchased product help](https://advertising.amazon.com/help/G9JR57B7AEWBQ3P2)). Unified reporting's converted product data is different: it holds promoted and halo products together (see `convertedProduct.id`).
- **Report cost and the invoice differ.** The cost column is click charges only, before tax. The invoice adds tax and any Regulatory Advertising Fee (maximum rates: 2% UK; 3% France, Italy and Spain; 5% Austria; 4.5% Turkey. The fee can apply where the ad is served or where the advertiser is based, so a UK-based seller can be charged both the UK fee and another such country's fee on the same click; [Regulatory Advertising Fees](https://advertising.amazon.com/help/GRQM2HSUXGUWFJ3H)), and takes off invalid clicks, over-delivery credits and promotions (it can also hold creator commissions; [Invoices](https://advertising.amazon.com/help/GDWV9R4RA7FCHSTE)). Invalid clicks can be removed up to 72 hours later, and late charges can land on the next month's invoice while the campaign data stays in the earlier month ([Understand campaign spend](https://advertising.amazon.com/help/GUJY2CG5DTCPDCVQ)). The invoice is what was charged, not always what was paid: check its status (Issued, Failed, Paid in part, Paid in full or Written off; [Invoices](https://advertising.amazon.com/help/GDWV9R4RA7FCHSTE)). For the cash actually paid and its date, use Transaction View's "Cost of Advertising" lines for ads paid from the seller account, or the card or bank statement for the rest. Never the report's cost.
- **The last few days are provisional.** In the campaign manager, Sponsored Products sales take up to 48 hours to fill in, and cost can change for up to 3 days ([Performance metrics](https://advertising.amazon.com/help/GG44RFW942U9F6F5)). Amazon's pages differ here: the Ads API overview says invalid traffic is watched for up to 30 days (see Recent days change), so don't treat a month's cost as final until then. Pull recent days again before relying on them.
- **History limits differ by report and surface,** and product-level data in unified reporting and v1 starts on 13 Feb 2025 ([Data availability](https://advertising.amazon.com/help/G8A5Z6UD9ME5W3GZ); see How far back Amazon goes). A range that starts earlier comes back with those product fields empty, not refused ([Data availability](https://advertising.amazon.com/help/G8A5Z6UD9ME5W3GZ)).
- **Some Sponsored Brands campaigns are missing.** v3 Sponsored Brands reports, still in preview, leave out campaigns whose `isMultiAdGroupsEnabled` is false ([v3 overview](https://d3a0d0y2hgofx6.cloudfront.net/en-us/guides/reporting/v3/overview.md)), and unified reporting may leave out legacy Sponsored Brands campaigns, the ones without ad groups: [Create a report](https://advertising.amazon.com/help/GK3FG568T93VE2VL) says they aren't supported at all, while [Understand unified reporting](https://advertising.amazon.com/help/GMH8A8AJSH4ATV6T) says only their targeting and search term reporting is missing, due later in 2026.
- **Throttling:** v3 and v1 return `429` when asked too often. v1 runs one report at a time per client ID, and a request for one account "can be throttled because of other requests made with the same client ID" ([v1 limits](https://d3a0d0y2hgofx6.cloudfront.net/en-us/guides/reporting/ads-v1/limits.md)). Amazon's reporting overview says the limit is per advertiser instead ([reporting overview](https://d3a0d0y2hgofx6.cloudfront.net/en-us/guides/reporting/overview.md)).
- **Mixed stores:** a report across stores mixes currencies and time zones. Total each currency separately, as in the other money reports.

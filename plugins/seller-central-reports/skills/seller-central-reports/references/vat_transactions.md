# Amazon VAT Transactions Report (`vat_transactions`)

Amazon's VAT Transactions Report for one month: sales, refunds and returns with their VAT detail, departure and arrival countries and VAT numbers, and now Amazon's own fee invoices and credit notes too, for the whole account in one file.

## Where to find it in Seller Central

**Supply chain > Supply chain reports**, the Amazon Fulfilment Reports page, then **Amazon VAT Transactions Report** under **Tax** in the left-hand list (it is also pinned under **Favourite**), at `sellercentral.amazon.co.uk/reportcentral/VAT_TRANSACTION/1` (checked in Seller Central UK on 28 Oct 2026). In classic Seller Central: **Reports > Fulfilment by Amazon**, then the same list. A banner in the Tax Document Library (Finance > Finance reports > Tax document library) links to it too.

Pick the **Event Month**, then request a .csv or .txt download. The table lists each request with the date range covered, the date requested, the file format and the status. Amazon's page says last month's report is ready on or after the third day of the current month. A banner says the report now includes fee data: filter `SALES_CHANNEL` for `AMAZON_FEE` and use `ACTIVITY_PERIOD`. One month took about a minute.

## Getting it through the API

- **Call / report type:** Reports API `reportType` `GET_VAT_TRANSACTION_DATA`, requested on demand. The pack's [report-catalogue](report-catalogue.md) lists it for "**UK** + EU" stores as "Amazon VAT Transactions Report: sales, returns, refunds, cross-border movements and FC transfers. **Available from the 3rd of each month.** Returns all stores regardless of `marketplaceId`."
- **Role:** **Tax Invoicing (restricted)**, plus a Restricted Data Token (RDT) to download it ([report-catalogue](report-catalogue.md); Amazon's [Tax Reports](https://developer-docs.amazon/sp-api/docs/report-type-values-tax)). The catalogue: restricted report types "need an RDT to call `getReportDocument`. The RDT must name the specific report-document path" ([Restricted Data Token](https://developer-docs.amazon/sp-api/docs/authorization-with-the-restricted-data-token)). Amazon reviews every role request "with additional scrutiny for restricted roles", and [getting-access](getting-access.md) advises leaving the restricted roles unticked. So most sellers download this report from Seller Central by hand.
- **How far back Amazon goes:** not stated in the pack's sources. Seller Central asks for one Event Month at a time; how far back it reaches wasn't checked.

## Columns

Seen in a real UK seller's Seller Central downloads (Oct 2026): a CSV with a UTF-8 byte order mark and LF line ends (the .txt choice wasn't checked), 95 columns, header in capitals, from `UNIQUE_ACCOUNT_IDENTIFIER` to `TAX_COLLECTION_RESPONSIBILITY`. The names below, in order, are Amazon's own list of the report's 95 attributes ([Tax reports attributes](https://developer-docs.amazon/sp-api/docs/tax-reports-attributes)), written in capitals as the file has them; all 95 match the real file's header, in the same order. That list gives names only, no meanings, so meanings here are by name. Find columns by name, not position.

**Dates are `DD-MM-YYYY`, with dashes**, unlike any other report in this pack. **Amounts are split three ways:** before VAT (`…_VAT_EXCL`), the VAT itself (`…_VAT_AMT`) and with VAT (`…_VAT_INCL`). Each block covers the items, delivery and gift wrap, each as the price, its promotion (`PROMO_…`) and the total after promotion (`TOTAL_…`), and ends with the whole line's total (`TOTAL_ACTIVITY_VALUE_…`).

## Personal data

`BUYER_NAME` (filled for business buyers), `ARRIVAL_ADDRESS`, `ARRIVAL_CITY`, `ARRIVAL_POST_CODE` and `BUYER_VAT_NUMBER` are the buyer's details. On `RETURN` rows the goods travel back from the buyer, so `DEPATURE_CITY` (Amazon's spelling) and `DEPARTURE_POST_CODE` are the buyer's location too: strip both on every row. Strip them before analysis and never copy them into anything shared. The seller's own VAT numbers and `UNIQUE_ACCOUNT_IDENTIFIER` are the seller's business details: keep them private too. Some sellers put their cost price in the SKU, so treat SKUs as private.

## Questions it answers

- How much VAT was charged on sales and refunds each month, per country (`TAXABLE_JURISDICTION`, the `…_VAT_AMT` totals; see the traps on which rows to count)? On standard VAT accounting, the VAT on the `SELLER` rows is Amazon's calculation of what the seller owes, so first check those rows have VAT filled in where VAT applies. If standard-rated UK sales show blank or zero VAT, don't treat that as no VAT owed: ask the seller whether they use Amazon's VAT Calculation Service, and work from their own invoices or accounting records instead. On the Flat Rate Scheme, the UK VAT the seller owes isn't these amounts: it's a flat percentage of their turnover, worked out on their VAT return, while VAT due in another country follows that country's rules; see [SKILL.md's VAT section](../SKILL.md#vat-included-or-not-per-file).
- Which sales did Amazon collect the VAT on (`TAX_COLLECTION_RESPONSIBILITY` `MARKETPLACE`; `TAX_REPORTING_SCHEME` names the scheme), and which must the seller account for (`SELLER`)?
- What VAT did Amazon charge on its own fees (`SALES_CHANNEL` `AMAZON_FEE`, by `ACTIVITY_PERIOD`; see the traps)? A VAT-registered seller on standard accounting can reclaim it; a Flat Rate Scheme or non-registered seller can't ([GOV.UK](https://www.gov.uk/vat-flat-rate-scheme/how-it-works); [SKILL.md's VAT section](../SKILL.md#vat-included-or-not-per-file)).
- Which sales went to business buyers with a VAT number? (Have the stripping script add a yes or no column for whether `BUYER_VAT_NUMBER` was filled, then drop the number.)
- Where did goods leave from and go to (`DEPARTURE_COUNTRY`, `ARRIVAL_COUNTRY`), and which were exports (`EXPORT_OUTSIDE_EU`)?

## Traps seen in real data

Seen in a real UK seller's Seller Central downloads (Oct 2026):

- **Fee rows can belong to the month before.** A month's file can carry fee rows (`SALES_CHANNEL` `AMAZON_FEE`) with the previous month's `ACTIVITY_PERIOD`. Group fees by `ACTIVITY_PERIOD`, as Amazon's banner says, not by the file they came in; a month's fees are probably in the next month's file.
- **`DEPATURE_CITY`** is Amazon's spelling. A search for `DEPARTURE_CITY` finds nothing.
- **The VAT rate is a fraction** (`0.2`), not a percentage, despite `_PERCENT` in the names. Don't divide by 100 again.
- **`COST_PRICE_OF_ITEMS` can be blank on every row,** so it can't be relied on for a cost price or for profit.
- **Dates are `DD-MM-YYYY` with dashes.** A date parser set up for another report's slashes or dots fails on them.
- **Byte order mark.** The file starts with one, so the first column name reads wrong until it is stripped.
- **One file for the whole account.** The form has no store choice, and the catalogue says the API version returns every store regardless of `marketplaceId`. Filter by `MARKETPLACE` or `TAXABLE_JURISDICTION` for one country, and by `TAX_COLLECTION_RESPONSIBILITY` for the sales the seller accounts for VAT on (see the next two traps).
- **Don't filter on `VAT_CALCULATION_IMPUTATION_COUNTRY`.** It can be blank on sale and refund rows, so a filter on it (e.g. `= GB`) silently drops those rows and their VAT. Filter on `TAXABLE_JURISDICTION`, `SALE_ARRIVAL_COUNTRY` and `TAX_COLLECTION_RESPONSIBILITY` instead.
- **`TAXABLE_JURISDICTION` isn't always where the VAT is owed.** It can say `UNITED KINGDOM` even on rows shipped to other countries where Amazon collected the destination's tax (named in `TAX_REPORTING_SCHEME`). For the sales the seller accounts for VAT on, take the rows with `TAX_COLLECTION_RESPONSIBILITY` `SELLER`.
- **Not before the 3rd.** Last month's report is ready from the third day of the month.
- **A CSV opened in a spreadsheet loses leading zeros** in SKUs and ASINs (Amazon's own warning on this page). Give Claude the file as downloaded, or ask for .txt ([seller-central-downloads](seller-central-downloads.md)).
